Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

Chillax ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Chillax mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Order Information: Details of menu items you order, order history, and preferences.
  • Room Booking Information: Gaming room reservations and session details.
  • Device Information: Device tokens for push notifications.
  • Loyalty Information: Points earned and redeemed through our loyalty program.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Manage room bookings and sessions
  • Send push notifications about order status and promotions
  • Manage your loyalty points and rewards
  • Improve our services and user experience

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For optional social login. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For push notifications. Subject to Firebase's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use encryption and authentication protocols to protect your personal information. Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

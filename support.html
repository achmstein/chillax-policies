Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Open the app, browse the Menu tab, add items to your cart, and checkout. You'll receive notifications as your order is prepared and ready.

How do I reserve a gaming room?

Go to the Rooms tab, pick an available room, and confirm your booking. You have 15 minutes to check in before the reservation is automatically cancelled.

How do I join a friend's session?

Ask your friend for the 4-digit session code, then tap "Join Session" in the Rooms tab and enter the code.

How does the loyalty program work?

You earn points on every purchase. As you collect points, you progress through Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers. Redeem your points for discounts on future orders.

How do I request services during a gaming session?

While in an active session, you can call a waiter, request a controller replacement, or ask for the bill directly from the app.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Your data will be permanently removed.
